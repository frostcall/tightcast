Help test TightCast

TightCast is a podcast player for Android that shortens dead air, levels volume and finds the ads in an episode, all on your phone. Before a new app can go on Google Play, Google requires a closed test with real testers. That's where you come in.

Email me to join

The button opens your email app with a message ready to fill in. If nothing opens, send your Google account address to sidelinesoundboard@gmail.com.

What you need

  • An Android phone running Android 10 or newer.
  • A Google account. The address you send must be the one signed in to the Play Store on that phone.
  • To keep the app installed for about two weeks, and listen to a few episodes with it.

How it works

  1. Email me your Google account address. Use the button above. It's the only thing I need from you.
  2. I add you to the tester list. Then I reply with an opt-in link.
  3. Open the link I send back. It's Google's opt-in page. Tap to become a tester.
  4. Install from Google Play. The app appears in the Play Store like any other app.
  5. Use it, and reply with what you find. Anything broken, confusing or missing. Short notes are fine.

Questions

What do you do with my email?
I use it only to add you to the test and to reply to you. I don't share it or add you to any mailing list. The Privacy Policy covers what the app itself does.

Can I bring my podcasts from another app?
Yes. Export an OPML file from your current app and open it with TightCast.

Is there an iPhone version?
No. It's Android only.

Can I leave the test?
Yes, at any time, from the same opt-in page or by uninstalling the app.
