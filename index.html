TightCast

A podcast player for Android with less to sit through · Coming to Google Play

TightCast is a full podcast player: subscribe, download, Up Next, playlists, folders, Android Auto and Chromecast. What sets it apart is what it takes out.

  • Dead air removed. Long pauses are shortened as you listen, streaming or downloaded.
  • Even volume. Quiet guests and loud hosts are leveled.
  • Starts at the start. It can begin an episode after the show's opening theme.
  • Ads found on your phone. It marks the ads in an episode and can skip them, with Undo every time.
  • Private by design. No account, no ads, no analytics. All of the listening and learning happens on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.
